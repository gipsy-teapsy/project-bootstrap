# Task Handoff

Use only when the Task result is ready for Master Review.

```text
HANDOFF

Result:
<goal-level outcome>

Changed resources:
<files, systems, or artifacts>

Verification:
<commands/checks and exact coverage>

Convergence:
<PASS or GAP against accepted requirements, with limitations>

Evidence:
<artifact or revision pointers>

Durable knowledge candidates:
<claims for Master to promote, revise, or reject>

Master request:
<review or decision needed>
```

Omit empty optional sections. Handoff does not itself promote knowledge, commit, push, merge, or release.
